SuJi 1.42.0

Onboarding / tutorial usability update based on new-user observation.

Level 1
- Added a delayed visual drag gesture coach.
- If a player taps a Rack shape without dragging it, the coach appears immediately.
- Coach message: "Drag shapes onto the board."
- Coach stops once the player successfully drags a Rack shape onto the Board.

Level 3
- After pressing Hint, instruction changes to: "Now tap a shape from the Rack."
- An animated finger points to a Rack shape and the Rack is visually emphasized.
- After selecting a shape, instruction changes to: "Now drag this shape to a highlighted position."
- During the mandatory first Hint lesson, pressing Hint again no longer cancels the lesson; it reinforces the next required action.
- Normal Hint cancellation remains unchanged outside the mandatory Level 3 lesson.
