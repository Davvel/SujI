SuJi Physical Print Builder v1.0

New in v1.0
- Padlock styling on the printed locked shapes has been reduced and refined to better match the digital game.
- Card 12 now shows only the locked starter-shape positions on the small board.
- Card 14 is a dedicated rules card explaining how to play and that padlocked shapes should be placed first.
- The set now prints 14 cards in total.

SuJi Physical Print Builder v0.9

New in v0.9
- Any shape that is locked in the level now shows a padlock on its own shape card.
- The padlock is centered on the printed shape, matching the board treatment.
- Card 12 now places each lock at the center of the locked shape, not just on a single cell.

SuJi Physical Print Builder v0.8

New in v0.8
- Card 13 is now a picture-only reference card.
- No Sudoku numbers are shown on Card 13.
- Card 13 title uses a friendly picture title, e.g. SuJi Face or Flag of Germany.

SuJi Physical Print Builder v0.7

Changes in this delta
- Cards 1-11 contain all printable shapes.
- Card 12 is now only the Quick-Start card:
  * miniature 9x9 board at the top,
  * ONLY the 3 actual digital-game locked starter shapes shown in their exact homes,
  * short rules underneath,
  * no complete picture on Card 12.
- Starter selection now mirrors the digital game's rack-order + seeded chooseAnchors logic exactly.
- Card 13 is a new Solved Board reference card showing the complete picture, Sudoku numbers, and 9x9 grid.
- Shape cards now display /13 in their header.
- Print button wording updated to 13 Cards PDF.

Important
The frozen level data remains the source of truth for published level numbers and geometry.
